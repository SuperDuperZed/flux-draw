# Flux Draw

The generative art canvas that lets you paint with glowing particle trails.

Interact: Click and drag (or tap and swipe on mobile) to paint. Switch between three visual modes - Ribbon, Nebula, and Shatter - each producing different particle effects on a dark background.

→ Live: https://superduperzed.github.io/flux-draw/

---

Part of the [Viral Engine](https://github.com/SuperDuperZed) experiment series.
